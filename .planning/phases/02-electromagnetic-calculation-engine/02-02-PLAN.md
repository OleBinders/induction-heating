<task>
<type>feature</type>
<description>Implement solenoid magnetic field on axis</description>
<read_first>
- /home/olebinders/Documents/Code/Induction Heating/.planning/phases/02-electromagnetic-calculation-engine/02-RESEARCH.md
</read_first>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Function: solenoid_b_field_on_axis(coil, current, z_positions) → B_z array
- Formula: B_z(z) = (μ₀NI/2) * [(z+l/2)/(l√(R²+(z+l/2)²)) - (z-l/2)/(l√(R²+(z-l/2)²))]
- Uses mean coil radius R = (inner_radius + outer_radius) / 2
- Supports array of z positions
- Returns B in Tesla
- Handles edge case: z = ±l/2 (at coil ends)
</acceptance_criteria>
<verify>
- At coil center (z=0), B ≈ μ₀NI/l for long solenoid (l ≫ R)
- At coil ends (z=±l/2), B ≈ μ₀NI/(2l) (half of center value)
- Far from coil (|z| ≫ l), B → 0
- Matches infinite solenoid formula when l ≫ R and z near center
</verify>
</task>
